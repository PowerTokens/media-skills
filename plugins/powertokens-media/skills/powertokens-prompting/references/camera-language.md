# Camera language that video models follow

Shot sizes: extreme close-up (detail, texture) · close-up (face/product) · medium (waist up) ·
full (whole body/object) · wide (subject in place) · extreme wide (scale).

Angles: eye level (neutral) · low angle (power, hero product) · high angle (vulnerable, flat-lay) ·
over-the-shoulder (conversation) · POV / first-person (UGC, unboxing) · top-down (food, desk).

Moves (name one per shot):
| Word | Effect | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| slow push-in / dolly in | builds attention | product reveal, realization |
| pull-back / dolly out | reveals context | ending, scale |
| orbit / arc around | shows form | product 360, hero |
| tracking / follow | continuity of action | walking, running |
| pan left/right | scan a space | establishing |
| tilt up/down | reveal height | buildings, outfits |
| crane up | release, scale | finale |
| handheld | urgency, authenticity | UGC, drama |
| static / locked-off | calm, readable | talking head, demo |

Lens and light words that help: "35mm", "macro", "shallow depth of field", "soft window light",
"golden hour", "neon rim light", "high-key studio", "overcast diffuse", "practical lamps".

Grade/style: "commercial look", "film grain", "teal and orange", "muted pastel", "high contrast
noir", "iPhone selfie footage" (UGC). Pick one; stacking styles dilutes them.
